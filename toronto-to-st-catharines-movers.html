HOW IT WORKS

The drive is about 130 km, usually 1.5 to 2 hours depending on traffic, and we unload at your new place.

What affects the price

We do not use one flat rate for long-distance moves. After you send the details, we reply with a price for your specific move.

  • Distance and travel time
  • Volume: how many furniture pieces and boxes
  • Access: stairs, elevators, long carries and parking
  • Date and time: weekends and the end of the month are busier
  • Extras such as furniture assembly

Who this is for

This service is built for small and mid-size moves.

  • Relocating from Toronto or the GTA to Niagara
  • Students moving to Brock University or Niagara College
  • Studio, apartment and condo moves
  • Furniture pickups and deliveries from Toronto to Niagara

Tips for moving out of a Toronto condo

  • Book the service elevator with your building as soon as you have a date
  • Reserve parking or a loading spot close to the entrance
  • Check the move-in rules and any deposit at your new building in Niagara too
  • Measure large furniture against doorways, stairs and elevators
  • Tell us about stairs and long carries when you ask for a quote, so the price is right from the start

Where we deliver in Niagara

We deliver to St. Catharines, Thorold, Niagara-on-the-Lake, Niagara Falls, Welland, Pelham, Grimsby and Lincoln. Moving somewhere else? Ask us and we will let you know if we can do it.

FAQ

Toronto to St. Catharines moving questions

How much does a move from Toronto to St. Catharines cost?

Long-distance moves are quoted individually. The price depends on distance, how much you are moving, building access and your date. Send us the details with the form on this page and we will reply with a custom quote.

How far is Toronto from St. Catharines?

About 130 km by road, usually 1.5 to 2 hours depending on traffic.

Do you handle small moves from Toronto?

Yes. We work with studios, one-bedroom apartments, condos, student moves and furniture pickups and deliveries.

Can you also move me from Niagara to Toronto?

Yes. We also quote moves from Niagara to Toronto and the GTA, and other destinations on request.

How do I get a quote?

Fill in the form on this page, or text or WhatsApp us at 416-837-2397. Tell us the pickup and delivery addresses, your moving date and what you are moving.

Do you assemble furniture?

Yes. Furniture assembly is one of our services. Mention it in your request so we include it in the quote.
